Row, every sport: headshot with team-logo badge · name + position · today's matchup and time · one headline stat on the right with its unit · green count of markets open (replaces the unlabelled "5").

The headline stat is the adapter's choice, not the component's: RB rush yds, QB pass yds, WR rec yds, MLB hitter OPS, pitcher ERA, golfer SG:Tot, NBA PPG. The row reads the same; only the number differs — the same rule as surface in the research cards.

Filters: position (multi), team, game, has-props-today, injury status; sort by the headline stat, name, or markets. Active filters render charcoal with a clear ✕.
