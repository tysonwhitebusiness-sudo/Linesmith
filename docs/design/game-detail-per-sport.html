One Game Detail, all eight

Third and last of the three shared pages. Same method: measure, then build it. Every tab renders the same nineteen blocks from the same template, no sport === 'x' in the renderer. Game Detail has three blocks neither of the other two pages has — a bookmaker grid, injuries, and the deciding matchup.

The three pages have different sport counts, and that is the real shape

Taken together the three studies produce a clean structural answer, and it is not “eight everywhere.” A tennis player has no team but plays a match. A golfer has neither, yet a tee-time pairing is already modelled in the codebase as liveMatchup.

Adapter presence read from lib/sports/*/adapters/; row counts measured 2026-08-29. Golf's game row is the one judgement call on the page — it has no gameDetailAdapter.ts, but PlayerDetailData.liveMatchup already carries a hole-by-hole scorecard against a tee-time groupmate, which is the same entity.

The finding that matters most: this is the leakiest of the three

GameDetailData carries eight sport-named fields — unitGrades (“NFL-only”), hero.awayGrades/ homeGrades (the same nine-field NFL TeamGrades), propsForGame (“NFL-only”), leftRail.nflTeamScope, hero.mlbLiveGame, hero.mlbGamePk, hero.liveExtraText (NFL's down & distance), and pregameLines.moneyline.draw (soccer's real third outcome — the one genuinely-earned exception on the list).

And one split that is an accident, not a design

CLAUDE.md §4 cites statComparison.bars (MLB) vs .ranked (NFL) as the model example of “genuinely different UI gets named mutually-exclusive fields.” Measured, it is not. MLB is the only sport that fills bars; NFL, CFB and soccer all fill ranked; and NBA, NHL and tennis fill neither, so the whole block is blank on three of seven pages. Meanwhile every one of the seven populates rankings, so ranks exist everywhere. That is the “leftover placement accident from the port” CLAUDE.md warns about in the same section — MLB was ported first and got bars; NFL came second and added a second shape rather than adopting the first. One ranked shape serves all seven and un-blanks three pages.

The bookmaker grid is the centrepiece of a Game Detail page, and it is the thinnest thing in the app. NBA and NHL have no rows at all; NFL, CFB and tennis have moneyline only from three or four books. This is the same class of gap as tennis on the player board and NFL spreads on the team board — a sourcing task, not a layout one.

The roles. matchupKey is the one carrying the most sport-specific content, and it exists on neither the player nor the team page.

What this means for the interface

  • Collapse statComparison to one shape. Drop bars, keep ranked, and have MLB's adapter emit ranked rows from the rankings data it already produces. One sport loses a bespoke rendering nobody asked for; three sports gain a block that is blank today. This is the single highest-value change on the page, and it makes CLAUDE.md §4's example wrong — so §4's example should be replaced with pregameLines.draw, which is genuinely earned.
  • Three blocks are game-only — bookGrid, injuries and matchupKey. An officials block was designed and then cut. A plate umpire's zone and a referee's card rate genuinely move markets, but a tree-wide grep returns zero hits for umpire, crew or officiating: it would have needed a separate new integration per sport with nothing reusable between them. Cut on 2026-08-29 rather than shipped as the one block with no path to real data.
  • hero.awayGrades/homeGrades and unitGrades take the same unitGrades fix proposed on the team board. That is three fields on two pages fixed by one type change, which is the argument for doing it before any of the chart work.
  • The mlbLiveGame escape hatch is the one to keep. A live MLB game genuinely needs bases, box score and bullpen state that no other sport has an analogue for. Keep it named and documented exactly as it is — but note that NFL, NBA and NHL all have real live states with nothing behind them today.
  • Book depth is the blocking gap for this page specifically. NBA and NHL have zero rows in game_odds_book_lines; NFL, CFB and tennis have moneyline only from three or four books. A bookmaker grid with three columns is not a grid. Fix sourcing before building the block, or it ships empty on five of eight tabs.
