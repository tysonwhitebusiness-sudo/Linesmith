Live replay (round 4). The page replays the last real hour of the scraper's recorded price history up to the snapshot, at 20× by default. Every flash, pull and new move you see is a change that really happened, at its real time. Ages tick every second. When the replay reaches the snapshot, the clock keeps running at 1× and no new data arrives, so you can watch sources go amber, then grey, as they age. "Checked" times during the replay follow each source's real poll interval (60–75 s for direct books, 45–90 s for aggregators, 5–10 min for splits). Green flash = the number went up (for a price, that means it pays more); red = it went down.
