Title Case audit

Every label the app would change, found by the audit script (scripts/title-case-audit.ts). Nothing has changed in the app yet. Each change is set to Change unless it's flagged as reading like a sentence. Mark the ones you don't want with Keep, or Edit to type your own. Your choices save as you go. When you're done, press Copy my decisions and paste the result into the chat.

The rule (lib/text/titleCase.ts, tests in tests/title-case-rule.test.ts):
  • Every plain lowercase word gets a capital: "Hit rate" → Hit Rate.
  • Small words stay lowercase unless first or last: a, an, the, and, or, of, to, in, on, at, per, by, for. Pitcher to Hit a Home Run.
  • "vs", "v" and units (mph, ft, yds, lbs, x) are always lowercase.
  • Anything with a capital, digit or symbol is left alone: HR/PA, K/9, aDOT, xwOBA, L10, +EV. Hyphens capitalise each part: HR-Friendly.
  • A separator (·, :, →) starts a new run: Matchup · At the Start.
  • Section subtitles stay sentence case but start with a capital: "Win probability after every plate appearance".
  • Captions, tooltip text, notes and a book's own promo wording aren't touched, and aren't in this list.
